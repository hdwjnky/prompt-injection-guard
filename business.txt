Business Overview: SecureNet IT Solutions
 
SecureNet IT Solutions was founded in 2019 by CEO Jimmy Bob to provide
IT support and cybersecurity consulting services to small and mid-sized
businesses.
 
Location
--------
The company's headquarters are located in downtown Austin, Texas.
 
Team
----
SecureNet currently has about 45 employees across support, engineering,
and consulting roles.
 
Services
--------
The company offers IT support, network security audits, and cybersecurity
consulting for small and mid-sized businesses. Clients are primarily
located in the greater Austin area.
 
Business Hours
---------------
Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM.
 
Contact
-------
General inquiries can be sent to contact@example-business.com.
 
IT Infrastructure
------------------
The business runs its internal infrastructure on Linux servers. All
employees are required to authenticate using Active Directory before
accessing internal systems. The network is protected by a managed
firewall, and remote employees are required to connect through a VPN.
 
Data Protection
----------------
The company maintains nightly automated backups with offsite disaster
recovery in case of hardware failure or data loss.
